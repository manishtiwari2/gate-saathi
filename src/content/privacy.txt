Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. About GATE Saathi

GATE Saathi is an AI-powered, neuroscience-informed educational platform that helps students prepare for competitive examinations such as GATE through organised study resources, practice, progress tracking, and AI-assisted doubt resolution. This Privacy Policy explains what data we handle and why.

2. Google Sign-In and Information We Receive

GATE Saathi uses Google OAuth (via Supabase Auth) as its sign-in method. When you click “Continue with Google,” Google shares a limited set of information with GATE Saathi solely to create and authenticate your account:

Your Google email address
Your full name
Your Google account profile picture (avatar)

GATE Saathi does not request — and does not receive — access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, calendar, or any other Google services beyond the basic identity profile listed above.

This information is used only to populate your GATE Saathi account (display name and avatar) and for authentication. You are not required to use Google Sign-In to use any free features of the platform.

3. Account and Profile Information

When you create an GATE Saathi account, we store the following information in our database:

User ID — a unique identifier generated by Supabase Auth (not your Google ID directly)
Username — a display name you choose during sign-up (visible to other users on leaderboards and chat)
Email address — used for authentication and account-related communication
Display name — derived from your Google profile name at sign-in
Avatar URL — link to your Google profile picture
Subscription tier — whether your account has active premium access

Your username is publicly visible on leaderboards and in study chat. No other personal information is shared publicly by default.

4. Study Activity and Progress Data

GATE Saathi tracks study-related activity to provide features such as verified study sessions, streaks, goals, and performance analytics. This includes:

Study session timestamps and durations (verified through page-visibility detection)
Daily study goals and streak history
Quiz and practice question responses and scores
Library resource access patterns (which resources you have viewed or bookmarked)
Leaderboard rankings based on verified study time

This data is stored securely in our Supabase database. It is used solely to operate and improve the platform's features.

5. Payment and Subscription Information

GATE Saathi uses Razorpay as its payment processor for subscriptions. Payment and billing details are handled entirely by Razorpay. GATE Saathi does not store your full card or bank details.

We receive and store limited subscription information from Razorpay:

Subscription status (active, expired, or cancelled)
Subscription plan type
Payment reference IDs (for support and dispute resolution)

For full details on how Razorpay handles your payment information, please refer to the Razorpay Privacy Policy.

6. AI Doubt Engine Data

GATE Saathi's AI Doubt Engine allows you to ask questions about GATE subjects and receive AI-generated answers grounded in your study library. When you use this feature:

Your question text is sent to our AI provider (Groq) for processing
Relevant context from your study library may be included in the request to produce accurate answers
The AI-generated answer is returned and displayed to you in real time

Doubt queries are processed dynamically and are not stored as persistent conversation history. However, usage of the AI feature may be logged for quality and improvement purposes. We do not use your doubt queries to train any AI models.

7. Global Study Chat

GATE Saathi offers a real-time study chat for premium users. Messages you send in the chat are visible to other users and are stored in our database (via Supabase) while the chat session is active. Do not share personal or sensitive information in chat messages.

8. Data Storage

All GATE Saathi user data — account profiles, study sessions, progress records, leaderboard entries, and chat messages — is stored using Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based cloud platform. Supabase provides encrypted data storage and transport. Our database configurations include Row Level Security (RLS) policies to enforce access controls at the database level.

9. Security Practices

GATE Saathi takes the security of your data seriously. Measures we implement include:

Authentication via Supabase Auth with secure session management
Database-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies
HTTPS enforcement for all connections
Service credentials stored as server-side environment variables and never exposed to clients
Limited data collection — we only collect what is necessary to operate the platform

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Third-Party Services

GATE Saathi integrates with the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

Supabase — authentication and database hosting
Google — OAuth sign-in provider
Groq — AI inference for the doubt engine
Razorpay — payment processing
Vercel — hosting and analytics

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Study progress and session data are retained to maintain your learning history and streaks.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us through our platform. Account deletion will remove your profile, study data, and associated records from our systems.

12. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you
Request correction of inaccurate data
Request deletion of your account and data
Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
Disconnect Google Sign-In from your GATE Saathi account at any time through your Google Account settings

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our platform.

13. Children's Privacy

GATE Saathi is intended for students and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact

For any questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out through our platform.
