Account and Profile Information\n\nWhen you create an PadhaiShuru account, we store the following information in our database:\n\nUser ID — a unique identifier generated by Supabase Auth (not your Google ID directly)\nUsername — a display name you choose during sign-up (visible to other users on leaderboards and chat)\nEmail address — used for authentication and account-related communication\nDisplay name — derived from your Google profile name at sign-in\nAvatar URL — link to your Google profile picture\nSubscription tier — whether your account has active premium access\n\nYour username is publicly visible on leaderboards and in study chat. Study Activity and Progress Data\n\nPadhaiShuru tracks study-related activity to provide features such as verified study sessions, streaks, goals, and performance analytics.
